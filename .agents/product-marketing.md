# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-06-08*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** The first intelligent shower system engineered to protect your skin and hair barrier from hard water.
**What it does:** Vellano offers a premium filtered shower system that neutralizes heavy metals and chlorine through a 3-step filtration process. It purifies water before it touches your body, tackling dryness, dullness, and buildup at their source rather than just treating surface symptoms. The product line includes the core showerhead, vitamin-C infusion attachments, filter refills, and hard-water body cleansers.
**Product category:** Water Filtration / Premium Shower & Bodycare
**Product type:** Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) E-commerce Hardware & Consumables
**Business model:** One-off hardware sales with recurring subscription revenue for filter refills and bodycare.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** N/A (Direct to Consumer)
**Decision-makers:** Individuals concerned with skin health, hair care, and home water quality.
**Primary use case:** Upgrading a standard home shower to a purified, spa-like experience that actively protects against hard water damage.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Protect skin barrier from chlorine and heavy metals.
- Prevent hard water buildup in hair (dullness, breakage).
- Elevate the daily shower ritual with premium attachments (Vitamin C).
**Use cases:**
- People living in hard water areas experiencing skin/hair issues.
- Beauty enthusiasts looking to optimize their routine from the source.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Standard tap water contains heavy metals, chlorine, and minerals (hard water) that strip the skin barrier and cause hair dullness/breakage.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Traditional skincare/haircare only treats the surface symptoms (moisturizing dry skin) without fixing the root cause (the water).
- Traditional water softeners are expensive, require plumbing, and aren't accessible to renters.
**What it costs them:** Wasted money on expensive beauty products that can't penetrate hard water buildup; chronic skin irritation.
**Emotional tension:** Frustration that despite an expensive beauty routine, skin and hair still look and feel unhealthy.

**Why that's better:** It creates a complete closed-loop defense against hard water.
